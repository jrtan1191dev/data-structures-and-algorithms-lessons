# Data Structures and Algorithms Resources

## Knowledge

- [NeetCode Roadmap](https://neetcode.io/roadmap)
  Interactive visual curriculum mapping core DSA topics and pattern hierarchies (Arrays & Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Stack, Trees, Graphs, DP). Use for: topic sequencing and curated problem progressions.
- [Tech Interview Handbook: Algorithm Study Cheatsheet](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/study-cheatsheet/)
  Curated cheatsheet by Yangshun Tay covering essential corner cases, common time/space complexities, and pattern recognition rules. Use for: quick revision and edge-case checklists.
- [Abdul Bari YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@abdul_bari/videos)
  In-depth foundational lectures on algorithm analysis, asymptotic notations, recursion, divide-and-conquer, greedy algorithms, and dynamic programming. Use for: deep conceptual intuition and rigorous theoretical grounding.
- [Video: "Fastest Way to Learn DSA (Even If your Logic is WEAK)" by Maddy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5QoGocSnjo)
  High-yield pattern-oriented approach to DSA focusing on pattern recognition over mindless grinding. Outlines the 30-minute daily practice rule, escaping the "tutorial trap," and building intuitive problem-solving muscle.
- [Video: "LeetCode was HARD until I Learned these 9 Patterns" by Maddy Zhang](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5QoGocSnjo)
  Deep breakdown of the 9 foundational interview patterns (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Hashing & Prefix Sums, Monotonic Stack, Binary Search, Trees/BFS/DFS, Intervals, Backtracking, DP) and how to identify visual triggers in problem descriptions.
- [Mental Framework: The Transition Mindset (Brute Force to Optimal) by Maddy Zhang](https://www.youtube.com/@maddyzhang)
  Structured 4-step interview problem-solving framework: (1) Constraint & complexity budgeting, (2) Naive brute-force state formulation, (3) Identifying redundant waste / invariant, (4) Selecting the minimal pattern data structure. Use for: tackling unseen interview questions under pressure.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [LeetCode Discuss](https://leetcode.com/discuss/)
  Active discussion forum where candidates share real interview questions, alternative algorithmic solutions, and optimization tradeoffs. Use for: seeing how other practitioners optimize and explain solutions.
- [r/leetcode](https://reddit.com/r/leetcode)
  Community of learners and engineers preparing for technical interviews, sharing study routines and troubleshooting conceptual hurdles. Use for: peer feedback, motivation, and difficulty benchmarking.
